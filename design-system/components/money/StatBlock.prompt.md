Big money figure with an eyebrow label — the total-profit headline and any KPI.

```jsx
<StatBlock label="Total profit" value="$14,820" delta="+$49 today" size="hero" />
<StatBlock label="MRR" value="$2,310" size="md" tone="gold" />
```

Pass `value` already formatted. `size="hero"` adds the green/gold text-glow. `tone`: `green | gold | plain`. `align`: `left | center`.
